AI for invention

Building AI Inventors.

How can we harness AI to drive humankind forward? What if AI was truly creative? How can we recombine the vast scientific literature and human expertise to create breakthroughs toward a healthier and more sustainable world?

01The thesis

Big leaps, not the nearest hill.

AI research agents have become very good at improving what already exists: tuning parameters, polishing a known solution, climbing the nearest hill.

They rarely step back to question fundamental assumptions, missing the uncharted, counterintuitive paths that lead to vastly higher peaks. That is where breakthroughs come from. We are building AI for invention: systems that make bigger leaps to find novel solutions, tested on real industry problems.

Point 01

Big leaps, not only fine-tuning.

Most AI agents polish an existing solution. We want new ways of looking at a problem.

Point 02

Real problems, not toy problems.

We test our work on problems that matter outside a benchmark.
