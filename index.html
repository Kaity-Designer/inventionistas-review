Building AI Inventors.

How can we harness AI to drive humankind forward? What if AI was truly creative? Systems that recombine the vast scientific literature and human expertise to create breakthroughs.

The thesis

AI research agents are very good at improving what already exists: tuning parameters, polishing a known solution, climbing the nearest hill.

They rarely step back to question fundamental assumptions, missing the uncharted, counterintuitive paths that lead to vastly higher peaks. That is where breakthroughs come from. We are building AI for invention: systems that make bigger leaps to find novel solutions, tested on real industry problems.
