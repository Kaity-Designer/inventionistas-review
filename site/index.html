The thesis

AI research agents are very good at improving what already exists: tuning parameters, polishing a known solution, climbing the nearest hill.

They rarely step back to question fundamental assumptions, missing the uncharted, counterintuitive paths that lead to vastly higher peaks. That is where breakthroughs come from.

We are building AI for invention: systems that make bigger leaps to find novel solutions, tested on real industry problems.

The autoresearch loop

AI that runs the research loop by itself.

  1. Propose

    Generate ideas

    It proposes hypotheses, recombining insight from distant and unconnected fields.

  2. Experiment

    Run experiments

    It designs and runs tests to put each idea under real pressure.

  3. Verify

    Check results

    It keeps what holds, discards what fails, and starts the loop again.
