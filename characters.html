Inventionistas / Character System

Five little monsters for an inventing machine.

A sticker set of cute, mischievous creatures built from the things Inventionistas is made of: the spark, the gear, the bulb, the brain and the bolt. Flat vector, bold rounded ink lines, brand violet and magenta with a cyan zap. Each is a tidy grouped SVG, ready to be rigged in Rive.
