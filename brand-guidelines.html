Clear space equals the spark height on every side. Keep that padding clear of other elements.

24 px / 0.25 in minimum

Minimum size for the icon is 24 px on screen, 0.25 in in print. Below that the spark loses definition.

Do

  • Keep the spark centered in the bulb.
  • Keep one base line under the bulb.
  • Use the mark on paper or on ink.
  • Match the icon weight to the text.

Don't

  • Add a gradient to the spark.
  • Recolour the bulb.
  • Rotate or skew the mark.
  • Add glows or shadows.
